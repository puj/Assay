# Assay — intro/usage video

Two deliverables:

1. **Generated demo (no narration)** — `marketing/assay-demo-vertical.mp4`
   (1080×1920, ~20 s, for TikTok/Instagram/Shorts) and
   `marketing/assay-demo-landscape.mp4` (1280×800, for the landing page,
   the store listing's YouTube link, X/Bluesky/LinkedIn). Rendered from the
   real extension driving a neutral mock chat; captions burned in. Regenerate
   with `npm run video` (needs `FFMPEG=<path to ffmpeg>`) after UI changes.
2. **Narrated version (record on the phone, ~40 s)** — script below. Screen
   record Firefox Android on a real ChatGPT reply; the generated cut is the
   storyboard.

## Narrated script

| t | On screen | Voice |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–4 s | A long ChatGPT reply, scrolling slowly | "Reading an AI answer is easy. Thinking with it is the hard part." |
| 4–9 s | Tap a word → highlight; tap again → sentence | "Assay. Tap a word. Tap again — the whole sentence." |
| 9–13 s | ＋ Add; second passage; ✎ Note, type "formalize this" | "Collect it. Add a few words if you want — before or after the quote." |
| 13–18 s | Tap a word in the next paragraph → span grows | "Grow across paragraphs, one word at a time. Every fragment keeps its own color." |
| 18–24 s | Open the Deep dive tray, scroll it | "Everything you kept, per conversation, while the chat stays right behind it." |
| 24–30 s | ↗ To composer; the message box fills; hit send | "One tap sends it all back. No boilerplate — your selection *is* the prompt." |
| 30–36 s | ⭳ .md download; file opens | "Or download the whole conversation, fragments included, as Markdown." |
| 36–40 s | End card | "Assay. Nothing leaves your device. Open source. assay.projectnothing.ai." |

## Shot notes

- Portrait, phone-native; don't rotate. Keep the system keyboard in frame
  during the note — it proves the focus fix.
- Use a reply with real substance (the "selection is the higher-bandwidth
  input modality" conversation is on-brand).
- Mute the site's UI sounds; add a light room-tone track under the voice.
