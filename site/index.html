Assay

Find what’s worth keeping in your AI conversations.

Reading an AI answer is easy; thinking with it is the hard part. Assay lets you tap the passages that matter while you read, annotate them in a few words, and send them back as one deep-dive prompt. Your selection is the signal — no boilerplate, no copy-paste, no API key, and nothing ever leaves your device.

The garbage collector becomes the allocator of life. Creators rarely bear the cost of rejection. The missing primitive is not annotation; it’s accumulating several annotations before responding. Selection is probably the higher-bandwidth input modality.

How it works

  1. 1

    Tap a word in any reply. Tap the highlight again to widen it — sentence, then paragraph. Tap other words in the same reply to grow the selection toward them, across paragraphs.

  2. 2

    ＋ Add collects it. ✎ Note attaches a few words — before or after the quote — now or later. Each fragment keeps its own highlight color on the page.

  3. 3

    The Deep dive tray holds everything you collected, per conversation, while the chat stays scrollable behind it.

  4. 4

    ↗ To composer writes just your fragments and notes into the message box. Review, send. Or download the whole conversation as Markdown or text, fragments included.

Private by design

No account. No server. No analytics. No API keys — your existing ChatGPT or Claude subscription does all the inference.

Fragments live in your browser’s local storage, per conversation. The only thing that ever leaves your device is the message you choose to send. The code is open source under MIT, so you can check.

Three ways to run it

  • Extension — Firefox (desktop and Android), Chrome, Edge. One tap from the store; updates itself.
  • Userscript — Tampermonkey or Violentmonkey: install from this link; it auto-updates too.
  • Bookmarklet — any browser, even iPhone Safari or Chrome on Android; tap it once per visit. Set it up here.
