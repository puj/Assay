## Title

Assay — Deep Dive for AI Chats

## Description

```
Reading an AI answer is easy. Thinking with it is the hard part.

Assay is a free, open-source browser extension for ChatGPT and Claude. Tap
any word in a reply to highlight it — tap again to widen it to the sentence,
then the paragraph. Tap other words in the same reply to grow the selection
toward them, even across paragraphs. Collect as many passages as you like,
add a few words of annotation to each, and send them all back as one
deep-dive prompt. No boilerplate is added — your selection is the signal.

What you get:
・Tap a word → highlight → sentence → paragraph → back
・Grow a selection across paragraph breaks by tapping toward other words
・Add a note before or after any quote, now or later
・Six rotating highlight colors, so you can see what you already took
・One tap sends your fragments and notes into the chat's own message box
・Export the whole conversation as Markdown or plain text, fragments included
・Every conversation keeps its own list

Private by design: no account, no server, no analytics, no API keys. Your
existing ChatGPT or Claude subscription does all the inference, and nothing
ever leaves your device except the message you choose to send.

Install: https://assay.projectnothing.ai
Source (MIT): https://github.com/puj/Assay
Privacy policy: https://www.projectnothing.ai/assay/privacy

Assay is not affiliated with OpenAI or Anthropic.

A Project Nothing experiment — https://www.projectnothing.ai/e/assay
This is an experiment. It might be gone next month.

#ChatGPT #Claude #BrowserExtension #Productivity #AI #OpenSource
```
