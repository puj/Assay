Winnow Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-09-02

Winnow is a browser extension that lets you collect, annotate and recombine passages from AI chat conversations. It is built to keep everything on your device.

What Winnow collects

Nothing. Winnow has no server, no account system, no analytics, no telemetry, and makes no network requests of its own.

Where your data lives

The passages you collect and the annotations you write are stored in your browser's local storage, on your device, scoped to the site you collected them from. They never leave your device unless you yourself send a composed message through the chat service you are using, or export a file to your own device.

What Winnow can access

The extension runs only on the chat pages it supports (chatgpt.com, chat.openai.com, claude.ai). It reads only the text you explicitly select or tap, and writes only into the page's own message box at your request.

Third parties

No data is shared with anyone, because none is collected. Your conversations with the AI services themselves remain governed by those services' own privacy policies.

Changes and contact

If this policy ever changes, the change will be visible in the extension's public repository history. Questions: hello@projectnothing.ai.
